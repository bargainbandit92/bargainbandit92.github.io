Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

BargainBandit ("we", "us") publishes short videos about shopping deals on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, and runs this website. This policy explains what information we handle.

Visitors to this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or sign-up forms, and we do not collect personal information from visitors. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to Amazon and other stores take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Our posting tools and third-party services

We use our own private software to publish our videos to our own accounts. It is used only by the BargainBandit team and is not offered to the public. Through it we connect to:

  • YouTube API Services, to upload our videos to our own YouTube channel. By using our YouTube content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) APIs, to publish videos to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account (Meta Privacy Policy).
  • TikTok, where we post to our own account (TikTok Privacy Policy).

These connections are authorized only for our own accounts. We access only what is needed to publish our own videos: we do not read, collect, store or share data about other users, viewers or followers. The access tokens for our accounts are stored securely on our own computer and can be revoked at any time, including through Google security settings and Facebook's Business Integrations settings.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and we do not sell or share it.

Data deletion

We do not collect or store personal data from people who log in with Facebook or use our content: our app is used only to publish to our own accounts. If you believe we hold any of your data, or you want it deleted, email bargainbandit92@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete anything we hold about you and confirm by email within 30 days. You can also remove our app's access to your account at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites.

Children

Our content is intended for adults shopping for deals and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions or requests: bargainbandit92@gmail.com.
